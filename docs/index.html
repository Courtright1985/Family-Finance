Family Finance

A private dashboard for our family to view our own bank balances and recent transactions. Bank connections are handled by Plaid. The app itself runs on our home server and is not a public product.

  • We only connect accounts that belong to our household.
  • Login happens in Plaid Link, not on this website.
  • Balances and transactions stay on our home server.
