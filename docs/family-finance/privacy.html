Family Finance

Privacy policy

Last updated August 13, 2026.

Who this is for

Family Finance is a private household dashboard. It is not offered to the public. The only users are members of our family.

What we collect

When we link a bank, Plaid sends us account names, masks, balances, and transactions for the accounts we choose. We store that information on our home server so we can see it in the dashboard.

We do not collect bank usernames or passwords. Those stay with Plaid and the bank.

How we use it

We use this data only to show our household its own balances and spending. We do not sell it, share it with advertisers, or use it to make credit decisions.

Plaid

Bank connections are provided by Plaid Inc. Plaid’s handling of data is described in Plaid’s End User Privacy Policy. You can manage or disconnect Plaid connections at my.plaid.com.

Where data lives

Dashboard data is stored on our home server. This GitHub Pages site is only a public description of the app for Plaid and the banks. It does not host account data.

Questions

Questions about this household app can be opened as an issue on the Family Finance repository.
